The program

Five steps up to your seat

Each cycle moves through the same five steps, from knowing where you stand to keeping the connections you make. Students of any major are welcome.

  1. 1

    Evaluation

    Take stock of your strengths, goals and where you want to go.

  2. 2

    Optimization

    Sharpen your resume, profiles and professional headshot.

  3. 3

    Cultivation

    Learn to start conversations and build real relationships.

  4. 4

    Application

    Practice with mentors, then network at a real event.

  5. 5

    Consolidation

    Follow up, keep your connections and take your seat.

Who it's for

Built for students starting out

  • High school and college students, in separate cohorts so everyone learns at the right level
  • Any major, and no business experience needed
  • Anyone ready to show up, put in the work and grow their network
